FAQs

What's included in the box?

The TCL 75" P7K comes with the TV panel, a detachable two-piece stand, a Bluetooth voice remote with batteries, a power cable, and a quick start guide. A wall-mount bracket is not included — you'll need a VESA 400 x 300mm mount, sold separately.

Does this TV support HDR gaming?

Yes. The TCL P7K supports Dolby Vision IQ and HDR10+, combined with a native 120Hz refresh rate, 0.1ms response time, and four HDMI 2.1 ports with ALLM (Auto Low Latency Mode). It's also compatible with NVIDIA G-Sync and AMD FreeSync Premium, making it well-suited for HDR gaming on PS5, Xbox Series X, and PC.

Can I use this TV without an internet connection?

Yes. You can connect external devices like a Blu-ray player, gaming console, or USB drive and watch content without Wi-Fi. However, the Google TV smart features — including app streaming, voice search, and Chromecast — require an internet connection to function.

What warranty does this TV come with?

This TV comes with a 3-year manufacturer warranty from TCL, covering defects in materials and workmanship. JB Hi-Fi also offers extended warranty options at checkout if you'd like additional coverage beyond the standard period.
